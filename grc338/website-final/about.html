Izakaya Yamato Ramen House is a family-owned restaurant that has been passed down through four generations since first opening in 1943 in the city of Guadalupe. Our restaurant specializes in traditional Japanese ramen made with homemade broths, fresh daily noodles, and carefully sourced ingredients from local farmers.

Yamato was created to bring authentic Japanese flavors to a small-town community in a setting that feels warm, calm, and welcoming. Whether guests are stopping in for a quick meal or enjoying a relaxed dinner, our goal is to offer food that feels both comforting and memorable.

The name Izakaya Yamato reflects our inspiration. “Izakaya” refers to a casual Japanese dining place, while “Yamato” suggests harmony and tradition. Together, the name represents the kind of experience we want every guest to have.
